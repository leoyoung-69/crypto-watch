The price already assumes a ~6x bigger category plus share retention, while Hyperliquid (HIP-4), Lighter (binaries since Oct 19) and Paradex compete for the same flow. Citrini holds a 10% weight. If you hold it, size small or hedge. Of the three "positions worth owning", HYPE (the hedge venue that earns fees on every re-hedge) carries less dilution and multiple risk.
2. **Buy time, not leverage, into the event cluster.** In Decentralised.co's backtest of BTC longs that finished in profit, a 10x perp was still liquidated along the way 47% of the time over 180 days (29% over 90d, 18% over 60d). In their replay of Oct 10, 2025, a 7-day put struck 5% OTM cost $52 per $1,000 of margin and kept a 10x long alive through the crash. On alts the same protection cost $250–340. With BTC vol low ahead of the Fed (next week), midterms (Nov 3) and refunding (Nov 4), dated calls or perp-plus-put structures beat naked leverage. Derive's BTC/ETH asks are within 0.2% of Deribit, but alt strikes (PUMP, LIT, ADA, VVV, CC) are mostly unquoted.

Compare the PT implied APY with STRC's cash yield and with Treasuries at >5%. Tokenized MSTR/STRC grew 40x in 12 months to a $508M mcap, and net mints of $432M add buy demand for MSTR.
